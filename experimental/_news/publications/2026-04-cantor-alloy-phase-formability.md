description: >-
  How closely do computational phase predictions match what happens in an alloy during heating? The team studied compositionally varied FeNiMnCr thin films with in-situ synchrotron X-ray diffraction, then compared the observed phases with predictions informed by density functional theory and CALPHAD. They developed a confidence metric to show where the models agree with the experiments and where they diverge. The comparison points to strong agreement in some regions and important FCC/BCC discrepancies in Mn-rich alloys, helping identify where future models need refinement.
