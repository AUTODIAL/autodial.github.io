# Congratulations to Taha on his Master's thesis defense!

Congratulations to [Taha]({{ '/group_members' | relative_url }}#mohammad-taha) on successfully defending his Master's thesis, *SEM Micrograph Representations: Robustness and Application to Synthesis-Microstructure Prediction*!

In his thesis, Taha explored how representations produced by prominent machine learning and statistical methods for SEM image analysis hold up to common types of noise encountered during SEM imaging. He also showed how representations from a vision foundation model can be used to establish a predictive relationship between synthesis conditions and microstructure.

Congratulations on this achievement, Taha!
